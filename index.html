About this site

Natural Remedy Daily is written and reviewed by our editorial team. We cite published research and official health guidance where claims are made, and we say so when the evidence is weak or absent.

How we write

  • We list the sources behind every article.
  • We say when evidence is limited or more research is needed.
  • We do not give medical advice or recommend products.

Nothing here is medical advice, and nothing here is a treatment. Herbs and supplements interact with prescription medication and are not appropriate for everyone. Talk to your doctor before starting anything, and do not stop prescribed treatment on the basis of anything you read here.
